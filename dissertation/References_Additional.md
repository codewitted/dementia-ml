## Citation Style Guide (Harvard Referencing)

### In-Text Citations

**Single author**: (Marcus et al., 2007) OR Marcus et al. (2007) demonstrated...

**Two authors**: (Lundberg and Lee, 2017)

**Three or more**: (Jack et al., 2010)

**Multiple citations**: ...machine learning in healthcare (Topol, 2019; Esteva et al., 2017; Gulshan et al., 2016)

**Page numbers** (for direct quotes): (Hastie et al., 2009, p. 337)

**Secondary citation**: Breiman (2001, cited in Hastie et al., 2009)

### Reference List Format

**Journal Article**:
Author(s) (Year). Title of article. *Title of Journal*, Volume(Issue), page range. DOI or URL

**Book**:
Author(s) (Year). *Title of Book* (Edition). Place: Publisher.

**Conference Paper**:
Author(s) (Year). Title of paper. In *Conference Name* (pp. page range). DOI or URL

**Online Resource**:
Organization (Year). *Title*. Available at: URL [Accessed Date].
